Codestash · access & organization model

Superadmin to Member

Four layers, start to finish: a platform-wide superadmin that sits outside every workspace, two ways to actually get a session, how a workspace and its members come into being, and what a role controls once you're in. This is the flow as it actually runs today — stubs included — plus two ideas on the table for later, kept clearly separate from what's real.

Worth knowing

  • Email codes are real, expiring 6-digit OTPs — but sendVerificationOTP only logs them to the server console today. No email provider is wired up yet, so this path only works for whoever has terminal access.
  • Accepting an invite is enforced server-side, not just by the link: the signed-in session's email has to match the invitation's exactly, or better-auth returns a 403.
  • There's no fallback for an unset active organization. use-auto-active-organization.ts only auto-selects one when you belong to exactly one workspace — anyone in zero or several has to pick, or every DB-backed read just comes back empty.
